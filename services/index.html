Bookkeeping services and packages

Everything that keeps your records accurate, current and ready for SARS — delivered every month at a fixed fee.

Bookkeeping services

Choose a package below, or tell us what you need and we will build the right mix.

Monthly bookkeeping

Every bank, card and cash transaction captured and coded to the right account — monthly, not in a year-end rush.

Bank & card reconciliations

Each account reconciled to its statement every month, with differences found and explained.

Debtors (money owed to you)

Invoices tracked and an age analysis showing who is overdue, so you know who to follow up.

Creditors (money you owe)

Supplier invoices captured and supplier statements reconciled, with a clear payment schedule.

Payroll records

Monthly payslips, payroll journals and the figures your EMP201 needs, kept in one place.

VAT-ready books

VAT coded on every transaction and VAT201 working papers prepared each period.

Monthly management reports

Income statement, balance sheet and a cash summary in plain language — what came in, what went out, what is left.

Catch-up & clean-up

Months or years of backlog captured and reconciled, so you start again from clean opening balances.

Cloud software setup

Set-up or migration onto cloud accounting software, with bank feeds connected so less is typed by hand.

Year-end pack

A reconciled trial balance and supporting schedules, ready for financial statements and tax at Think Mybiz.

What you send us

  • Bank and card statements, or access to bank feeds
  • Sales invoices and supplier invoices for the month
  • Cash slips and petty-cash records
  • Payroll changes: new staff, leave, overtime, terminations
  • Anything unusual: loans, asset purchases, owner payments

What you get back

  • Reconciled bank and card accounts
  • An up-to-date debtors and creditors list
  • VAT201 working papers (VAT vendors)
  • Payroll records and EMP201 figures (employers)
  • A monthly report with questions for you, if any
