About us

Bookkeeping you can rely on, inside a registered practice

Banda Bookkeepers is the bookkeeping desk of Think Mybiz Accountants and Tax Practitioners — one practice, one team, one point of accountability.

Who we are

We keep small-business books clean, current and reconciled — every month, not just at year-end. We capture transactions, reconcile bank accounts, track who owes you and who you owe, and keep your records ready for VAT, payroll and SARS.

Because we are part of Think Mybiz Accountants and Tax Practitioners, your books flow straight into tax returns, financial statements and compliance at the same practice. Nothing is lost between a bookkeeper and an accountant.

Mission: give every small business owner books they can trust and understand, closed off every month.

Vision: to be the bookkeeping partner South African small businesses recommend first.

Our values

  • Accuracy first — every account reconciled, every balance explained.
  • On time, every month — fixed monthly deadlines, not year-end catch-ups.
  • Plain language — reports owners can read without an accountant beside them.
  • Confidentiality — records handled privately and in line with POPIA.
  • Evidence-based — every entry supported by a statement, invoice or slip.
