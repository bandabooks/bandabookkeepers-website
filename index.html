Why monthly matters

Year-end bookkeeping is expensive bookkeeping

When books are left until year-end, missing slips are harder to find, bank differences pile up and VAT returns are prepared from guesses. Fixing twelve months at once costs more than keeping one month right.

Monthly bookkeeping means problems surface while they are small, VAT is supported by real records, and you can see your cash position before you make a decision — not after.

Questions

Bookkeeping FAQs

What does monthly bookkeeping cost?

We quote a fixed monthly fee after a free consultation. The fee depends mainly on how many transactions you have each month, how many bank and card accounts you use, and whether you need payroll records or VAT working papers.

Do I need a bookkeeper if I already have an accountant?

Your accountant prepares financial statements and tax returns from your records. A bookkeeper keeps those records accurate every month. At Banda Bookkeepers both sit in the same practice: we keep the books, and Think Mybiz Accountants handles tax and financial statements.

Can you catch up books that are months or years behind?

Yes. A clean-up captures and reconciles the backlog, confirms opening balances and lists anything that needs your input. Once the books are current, we move you onto a monthly package.

Do you submit my VAT and tax returns?

Bookkeeping prepares VAT-ready books and VAT201 working papers. Submissions to SARS, tax returns and financial statements are handled by our main firm, Think Mybiz Accountants and Tax Practitioners.

Do I need to visit your office?

No. Most clients send documents electronically and we work remotely across South Africa. You are welcome to meet us at our Mokopane office or arrange a meeting in Polokwane, Pretoria, Centurion, Johannesburg or Midrand.

Which accounting software do you work with?

We work with the cloud accounting software you already use, or help you choose and set one up, with bank feeds connected where your bank supports it.
