7 signs your books are behind — and how to catch up

Unreconciled bank accounts, guessed VAT and a shoebox of slips. How to tell your books need attention, and what a clean-up involves.

Banda Bookkeepers · Updated 21 November 2026

Books rarely fall behind all at once. A busy month becomes a busy quarter, and suddenly nobody is sure what the real bank balance is. Here are the warning signs.

  1. Your bank accounts have not been reconciled for more than a month. Without reconciliations you cannot be sure the books are complete.
  2. VAT returns are prepared from bank statements alone. VAT should be supported by valid invoices, not estimated from what left the account.
  3. Slips and invoices live in a box, a car or a WhatsApp chat. If documents are not filed as you go, they go missing.
  4. You do not know who owes you money. An out-of-date debtors list means late payers are not chased.
  5. Your accountant asks the same questions every year. Recurring year-end queries usually point to month-end gaps.
  6. You cannot answer a bank or funder quickly. Loan applications and tenders ask for recent management accounts.
  7. A SARS letter has arrived and the support is hard to find. Records you cannot produce are records you do not have.

How a clean-up works

A catch-up or clean-up project brings the books back to a reliable position:

  1. Collect bank statements and available documents for the backlog period.
  2. Capture and code every transaction.
  3. Reconcile every bank and card account, month by month.
  4. Confirm opening balances and list items that need your input.
  5. Move onto a monthly package so it does not happen again.

If a SARS letter is part of the picture, the tax side is handled by our main firm — see SARS letters and disputes at Think Mybiz.
