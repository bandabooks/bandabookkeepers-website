What we collect through this website

When you submit the enquiry form we receive the details you enter: your name, email address, phone number, business name and town, and your answers about your business and bookkeeping needs. This website does not use advertising cookies or analytics trackers.

Why we use it

Only to respond to your enquiry, prepare a quote and, if you become a client, to provide the services you engage us for. We do not sell your information or use it for marketing without your consent.

Where it goes

Form submissions are emailed to the practice mailbox and are not stored on the web server. If you become a client, your records are kept as required by law and our professional obligations.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or object to its use, by emailing accounts@thinkmybiz.co.za. You may also lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa).
